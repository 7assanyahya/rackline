# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Rackline to be a welcoming project for everyone, whatever their age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, nationality, appearance, race, religion or sexual orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that helps:

- Being kind, patient and respectful, including when you disagree
- Giving and accepting constructive feedback gracefully
- Focusing on what's best for the people who use Rackline
- Helping newcomers find their way

Examples of behaviour that isn't acceptable:

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks
- Sexualised language or imagery
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission
- Any other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions) and whenever someone represents the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainer through a private message on GitHub ([@7assanyahya](https://github.com/7assanyahya)). Reports are reviewed promptly and kept confidential. Maintainers may remove comments, commits or other contributions, or temporarily or permanently ban anyone whose behaviour breaks this code.
